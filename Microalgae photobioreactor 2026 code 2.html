Design Concept: Cascade Parallel-Plate PBR (Model 15-31)

This design transitions from the simple stacked stack implied by the hand-drawn sketch to a modular, cascade parallel-plate photobioreactor (PBR) system. The main design goals are high surface-area-to-volume ratio, optimized light penetration, and scalable modularity.

1. Core Structure & Materials

The main reactor body (Box 1) is an assembly of stacked parallel plates (the "Total 15-31 layers" stack). It is constructed from highly transparent, rigid materials: 2-3 inch transparent Polystyrene or Polycarbonate, or reinforced glass. Each internal chamber (Photo-bioreactor 1) maintains a precise 36-inch x 36-inch footprint and a internal culture thickness (1/2 inch per channel), as detailed in the dimensional annotations of Figure 15.

2. Layer-by-Layer Functionality

Referencing the cross-section diagram (bottom-left of the blueprint), the chambers are composed of functional layers:

  • (1) Transparent Cover Plate: Protects the system while allowing light transmission. (Details from Fig 15).
  • (2) Grid/Mesh Plate: Promotes uniform flow distribution and introduces mild turbulence for mixing.
  • (3) Aerator System: Points to the specific integration of the 'pipe areator' system detailed in Fig 15. The system delivers CO2-enriched gas.
  • (4) Algae Culture Layer: The primary growth medium and culture. This layer's serpentine flow is critical.
  • (5) Light Diffuser Layer: A crucial advanced feature that evenly distributes light received from the Light Sources to prevent light limitation and hot spots.
  • (6) Flow Channel: A defined path that guides the medium for maximum exposure to light and mixing.

3. Light Management System

The design incorporates all four main 'Light Source' arrays indicated in your sketch, positioned above, below, and alongside the stack. We explicitly define the parameters using the data from Figure 15: We utilize a total of 6 specific lights, each delivering 3000 lumens, ensuring a high photon flux density across the large surface area of the parallel plates.
